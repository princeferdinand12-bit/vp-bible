❓ FAQ

Common questions answered

How do I download Scripture for offline use? ›
VP Bible downloads Bible translations automatically for offline access. Go to Settings → Bible Translations → Download Offline Copies. Select your preferred version and it will download in the background.
Can I highlight verses and add notes? ›
Yes! Right-click any verse to highlight or add a note. Use Ctrl + B to bookmark. All notes sync across your devices if you're logged in to your VP Bible account.
Is my data private and stored securely? ›
Absolutely. All your personal notes, highlights, and bookmarks are encrypted and stored securely. We use industry-standard AES-256 encryption. Your data is never shared with third parties.
